Empower People – Incident Reporting & Family Reconnection Portal

Welcome.

This secure portal enables individuals, families, community members, NGOs, and authorities to report missing persons, found persons, suspected trafficking, forced marriage, and request assistance with family reunification.

Information submitted through this portal will be treated confidentially and used only for case management, referrals, and humanitarian support.

Submission of this form does not replace reporting to the Police. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the nearest Police Station or Dial 112 immediately.
